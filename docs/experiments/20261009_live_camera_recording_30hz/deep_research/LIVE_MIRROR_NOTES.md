Hook choice is deliberate: `_capture_new_observation` returns canonical `(RecordedObservationToken, frames)` after the original sampler capture and canonical hash binding. Wrapper reads THAT same copied frame and freezes matrices into bytes. No extra sampler call, no extra live state reads, no D0/action replacement. `capture_observation` promotion of prior successor does not call the hook, so it does not duplicate images. N committed transitions normally produce N+1 observations (initial O0 and terminal ON); count images per source observation, and action Hz separately. Rejected observations may still have images; join with finalHDF committed source IDs, do not imply every rendered observation is committed.

IPC: inherited private socket, trusted same-user parent/child pickle only, no network listener or external RPC. Parent retains at most8 immutable payloads until worker ACK. It blocks rather than overwrites/drops when full. Worker reads one snapshot at a time and renders all3camera products before ACK. That ACK means source snapshot consumed and3frames submitted to owned NVENC queues; it is NOT final bitstream completion. Each encoder separately holds up to8 allocations until packet ordinal ACK. Finaldrain checks all. Total potential source-to-compressed latency includes both queues; producer ACK age is not full packet latency. Retained worker rows and packet ledgers permit final join.
